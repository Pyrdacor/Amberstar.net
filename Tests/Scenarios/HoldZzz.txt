# Holding Zzz (the mouse button on the middle button or key 5 of the numeric keypad) lets the time pass like in the original.
# One step is 5 minutes. The first repeat is after 0.3 seconds, then every 0.1 seconds.
waittext
dismissall
buttonlayout Movement
# --- key 5 of the numeric keypad held for 2 seconds: about 19 steps
settime 8 0
hour
hold Keypad5 2000
hour +1
# --- the mouse button held on the middle button
settime 8 0
hour
holdmouse 256 169 2000
hour +1
# --- also on 3D maps
teleport 67 7 15 down
dismissall
settime 8 0
hour
hold Keypad5 2000
hour +1
settime 8 0
hour
holdmouse 256 169 2000
hour +1
