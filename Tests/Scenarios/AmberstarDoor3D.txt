# With the Amberstar the party walks through doors on 3D maps too and stays on the door like in the original
# (Door in the program returns at once with the Amberstar). The door of the entrance of Godsbane is at 27,42 of map 148.
waittext
dismissall
# --- without the Amberstar the door screen opens and the party is in front of the door after closing it
teleport 148 27 43 up
dismissall
wait 1000
move up 1
waitscreen Door
closeall
waitscreen Map3D
pos 27 43
# --- with the Amberstar the party walks onto the door and through it
setspecialitem Amberstar 1
wait 1000
move up 1
waitidle
screen Map3D
pos 27 42
move up 1
waitidle
pos 27 41
